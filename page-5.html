CNA Samples

Microsoft's XNA 4.0 samples, ported to C++ with CNA and compiled to WebAssembly. Every sample below runs in this browser on WebGL 2 — no plugin, no download.

  • Simple Animation

    Watch a tank's wheels, steering, turret, cannon and hatch animate through model bones.

  • Custom Model Animation

    Play animations baked into a rigid cube and a skinned walking character.

  • Custom Model Class

    Render a rotating tank with a custom model class and content processor.

  • Custom Model Effect

    Render a rotating saucer with a custom effect and photograph-derived reflection map.

  • Skinning Sample

    Animate a skinned character with the sample's model processor and animation player.

  • Skinned Model Extensions

    Turn animated bones, attach a baseball bat and inspect moving collision spheres.

  • CPU Skinning

    Switch between CPU and GPU skeletal animation of the same textured character.

  • Inverse Kinematics

    Move a cat and watch a 20-link chain reach it using cyclic coordinate descent.

  • Chase Camera

    Fly a ship and compare a spring-based chase camera with a fixed-offset camera.

  • Audio 3D

    Hear a moving cat and looping dog through distance, stereo positioning and Doppler pitch.

  • Sound and Music

    Play a sound effect, control a looping sound, and play a song with touch or mouse input.

  • Marble Maze

    Tilt a 3D maze to guide a marble around walls and pits toward the finish.
