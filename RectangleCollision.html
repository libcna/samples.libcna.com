About this sample

Move the person beneath falling blocks. The background turns red whenever the two sprites' rectangular bounds overlap. Their visible shapes can miss each other and still count as a hit. This first tutorial uses Rectangle.Intersects; the per-pixel tutorial tests the opaque texture pixels instead.
