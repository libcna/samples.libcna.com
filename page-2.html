CNA Samples

Microsoft's XNA 4.0 samples, ported to C++ with CNA and compiled to WebAssembly. Every sample below runs in this browser on WebGL 2 — no plugin, no download.

Samples 13–17 of 17

← Previous 1 2

  • Spacewar

    Two-player starship combat in classic Retro lines or textured Evolved 3D.

  • Bounce

    One hundred lit spheres, implicit collision response and gravity controlled by tilt or arrow keys.

  • Collision Sample

    Animated triangle and oriented-box collision tests alongside XNA's geometric primitives.

  • Per-Pixel Collision

    Falling blocks only hit when their visible pixels touch the player, not just their rectangles.

  • Rectangle Collision

    A falling block counts as a hit when its rectangle overlaps the player's, even if their visible pixels miss.
