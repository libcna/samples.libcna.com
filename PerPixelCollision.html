About this sample

Move the person under falling blocks. The background turns red only when opaque pixels of the two sprites overlap. Their rectangular bounds can overlap while the visible shapes miss. The sample reads the original texture pixels through Texture2D.GetData and tests the shared area, pixel by pixel.
