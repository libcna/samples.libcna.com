About this sample

Hold an empty spot to create a cat. The sample recognizes Hold, Tap, Double Tap, Free Drag, Flick and Pinch, and uses them to manage the cat sprites.

Controls

ActionControl
Create or remove a catHold an empty spot or a cat
Change colorTap or double tap a cat
Move or throwDrag or flick a cat
ResizePinch with two fingers
