Opens in a new tab. Start with Space or a touch, then catch snowflakes before time runs out. The picture shows the game after a round has begun.

About this sample

Steer a shovel through falling snowflakes in a timed arcade round. Catching snowflakes earns points and plays a sound. When the round ends, start another to try again.
