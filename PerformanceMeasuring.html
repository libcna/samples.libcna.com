About this sample

Colored spheres bounce above a checkerboard floor. The FPS counter and time ruler show the cost of updating and drawing the scene as the number of spheres and their interactions change.

Controls

ActionControl
Increase or decrease spheresHold Up or Down
Toggle sphere collisionsPress X
Open the debug consolePress Tab
Try System Link discoveryType remote in the console and press Enter
ExitPress Escape
