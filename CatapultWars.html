About this sample

Launch boulders at the other catapult in a turn-based duel. The game includes wind, animated catapults, a phone opponent, sound effects, instructions and a pause menu.

Controls

ActionControl
Start and continueTap or click Play and the instructions screen
Aim and fireDrag on the game field and release
PauseEscape; choose Return or Quit Game in the pause menu
