About this sample

A normal map makes the lizard and rock appear detailed without adding geometry. The original content processor generates tangent and binormal vertex data, assigns a custom compiled effect to each material, and converts normal textures to a signed format. Move the camera to inspect how the animated light changes the surface highlights.
