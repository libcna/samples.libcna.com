CNA Samples

Microsoft's XNA 4.0 samples, ported to C++ with CNA and compiled to WebAssembly. Every sample below runs in this browser on WebGL 2 — no plugin, no download.

Samples 13–24 of 47

← Previous 1 2 3 4 Next →

  • Spacewar

    Two-player starship combat in classic Retro lines or textured Evolved 3D.

  • Bounce

    One hundred lit spheres, implicit collision response and gravity controlled by tilt or arrow keys.

  • Collision Sample

    Animated triangle and oriented-box collision tests alongside XNA's geometric primitives.

  • Per-Pixel Collision

    Falling blocks only hit when their visible pixels touch the player, not just their rectangles.

  • Rectangle Collision

    A falling block counts as a hit when its rectangle overlaps the player's, even if their visible pixels miss.

  • Transformed Collision

    Rotated falling blocks only hit when their visible pixels touch the player.

  • Transformed Collision Test

    Drag, rotate and scale two sprites to explore transformed per-pixel collision.

  • Path Drawing

    Draw a route from the tank with touch or mouse and watch it follow the waypoints.

  • Pathfinding

    Compare three route-finding algorithms across four maps, then watch the tank follow the path.

  • Waypoint Navigation

    Place waypoints for a tank, then compare direct movement with smooth steering.

  • Flocking

    Tune how birds gather, separate and flee from a cat.

  • Chase and Evade

    Move the cat while a tank chases it and a mouse flees or wanders nearby.
