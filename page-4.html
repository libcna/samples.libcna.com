CNA Samples

Microsoft's XNA 4.0 samples, ported to C++ with CNA and compiled to WebAssembly. Every sample below runs in this browser on WebGL 2 — no plugin, no download.

Samples 37–48 of 58

← Previous 1 2 3 4 5 Next →

  • Shadow Mapping

    Move the camera around a character and watch its shadow map follow the visible scene.

  • Billboard Sample

    Walk through wind-swayed grass and trees drawn as camera-facing billboards.

  • Instanced Model

    Compare three ways to render a thousand animated model copies, including hardware instancing.

  • Lens Flare

    Explore a lit mountain landscape and move the camera around the sun.

    Known WebGL 2 limitation: the sun glow and lens flares are not visible.

  • Shatter Effect

    Hold a key to scatter a tank into individual triangles, then bring it back together.

  • 3D Particles

    Switch between explosions, smoke plumes and rings of fire, then orbit the camera around the scene.

  • 2D Particles Pipeline

    Switch between explosions, smoke plumes and a movable emitter driven by compiled particle settings.

  • XML Particles

    Explore explosions, smoke and fire whose particle settings come from compiled XML files.

  • 3D Graphics

    Rotate and zoom a spaceship, switch its three lights, and explore backgrounds and animation.

  • Picking Sample

    Point at a model to identify it while the camera circles a textured table.

  • Triangle Picking

    Point at a triangle to highlight its edges and orbit the camera around the models.

  • Heightmap Collision

    Roll a sphere over terrain generated from a heightmap while the camera follows.
